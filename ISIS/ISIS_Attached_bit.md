Attached bit

 The 4-bit Attached field is set by Level 1/2 routers in their Level 1 LSPs

 to indicate to same-area Level 1 routers that they are connected in other areas


R1 (L2) ----   R2  (L1/2)---- R3 (L1)
	   <Area 45>		<Area 49>
